# 75 Challenge

A personal 75-day challenge companion built with Expo and React Native.

## Included

- Three 25-day phases with step and workout targets
- Two breakfast, snack, and dinner choices per phase
- An A/B meal rotation for every day
- Daily check-ins for meals, steps, and workouts
- Local on-device progress storage
